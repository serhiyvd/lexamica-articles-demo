For most plaintiff firms, referrals are the single largest source of high-value cases — and the least predictable. A great case comes in through a relationship, a conference handshake, or a returned favor, and then the pipeline goes quiet for weeks.

The firms that scale don't leave this to chance. They turn referrals into a repeatable operation.

## Why referrals stay unpredictable

Three things keep most firms stuck:

- **No visibility.** Once a case leaves your firm, you lose sight of what happens to it.
- **Reliance on individuals.** When the person who owns a relationship leaves, the pipeline leaves with them.
- **No accountability.** Nothing structurally holds the receiving firm to status updates, timelines, or fee terms.

## What a system looks like

A predictable referral pipeline has three properties: cases are matched to the right counsel, every case is tracked to resolution, and fees clear cleanly without chasing. When those three are in place, referral volume stops being a function of who you know and starts being a function of how you operate.

## The takeaway

Predictability comes from process, not relationships. Build the process, and the relationships compound on top of it.
