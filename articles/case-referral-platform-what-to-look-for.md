Choosing a case referral platform is a decision you live with for years. The wrong one becomes a directory you ignore; the right one becomes the operating system for your referral business. Here's what separates the two.

## 1. Match quality, not just volume

A flood of low-fit cases wastes everyone's time. The platform should route cases to the right counsel by case type, jurisdiction, and capacity — not just broadcast them to the highest bidder.

## 2. Visibility after the handoff

You should be able to see what's happening to a case after it leaves your firm: status, milestones, and timelines. A platform that goes dark after the referral is just a lead list.

## 3. Accountability built in

The best platforms hold both sides to structured obligations — response times, status updates, and fee processing — so trust is structural rather than personal.

## 4. Clean fee handling

Referral and co-counsel fees should clear through the platform without spreadsheets and chasing. If you're still reconciling payments by hand, the platform isn't doing its job.

## The takeaway

Volume is easy to advertise and hard to make useful. Evaluate platforms on match quality, post-handoff visibility, accountability, and fee handling — that's where the real value is.
