In a personal injury case, the medical record is the evidence. It establishes the injury, the treatment, the cost, and the link back to the incident. Yet collecting and organizing those records is one of the most time-consuming parts of the case.

## What the records need to show

- **Causation.** A clear link between the incident and the injury.
- **Treatment.** What care was provided, and when.
- **Cost.** The bills that support the damages claim.

## Where the process breaks down

Records come from multiple providers, in different formats, on different timelines. Chasing them by phone and fax delays cases and frustrates clients. Automating record and bill collection — connecting directly to providers — removes weeks from the timeline.

## The takeaway

Strong medical documentation is the backbone of the claim. The faster and cleaner you collect it, the stronger and quicker the resolution.
