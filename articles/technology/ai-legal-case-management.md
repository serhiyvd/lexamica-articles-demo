Artificial intelligence has moved from buzzword to back office. The firms seeing real gains aren't chasing flashy features — they're applying AI to the slow, repetitive parts of case management.

## Where AI is actually useful

- **Intake triage.** Classifying and routing new cases by type, jurisdiction, and value in seconds.
- **Status summarization.** Turning scattered updates into a clear picture of where every case stands.
- **Document review.** Surfacing the relevant records from a mountain of files.

## Where to be careful

AI is a strong assistant and a poor decision-maker. Keep a human in the loop for judgment calls — fee terms, strategy, and anything client-facing.

## The takeaway

Use AI to remove busywork, not to replace judgment. The firms that get this balance right move faster without losing control.
