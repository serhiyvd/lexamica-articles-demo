The gap between firms that scale and firms that stall is increasingly a technology gap. Here are the five tool categories that define a modern plaintiff practice in 2026.

## 1. Case management
The system of record for every matter. Non-negotiable.

## 2. Referral & co-counsel platform
Where inter-firm cases are matched, tracked, and paid — the layer generic case management was never built for.

## 3. Medical coordination
Connecting clients to providers and automating record and bill collection.

## 4. Document automation
Generating agreements and filings from templates instead of by hand.

## 5. Analytics
Knowing which case types, sources, and partners actually drive revenue.

## The takeaway
You don't need every tool — you need the right five, integrated, so cases flow between them without manual re-entry.
